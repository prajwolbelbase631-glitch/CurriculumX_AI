The teacher

Who's behind OPT MATH

For more than 25 years, Padma Prasad Acharya has taught Optional Mathematics to students preparing for their SEE and board exams — first in the classroom, and now to a wider audience through the OPT MATH YouTube channel.

His notes follow the same step-by-step method he uses in his videos: no shortcuts skipped, no working hidden — built for students who want to actually understand a method, not just memorise an answer.
